# Databricks at Scale

### What this is, in one page

---

Ask three teams for last month's churn rate and you'll get three different numbers.

Nobody's lying. Nobody wrote bad SQL. They mean three different things by the same word — and every decision built on that number inherits the confusion.

I spent a big part of my recent work on exactly that problem: a model that predicts which subscribers are about to cancel, across eight markets in a streaming business, running every day on Databricks.

The model turned out to be the easy part. This series is about everything around it.

---

## What I'm doing

Telling the story of that project in **9 episodes**, in public.

Each episode takes one real moment from the build — a problem, a decision, sometimes a mistake — and explains the part of Databricks that solved it, in plain English.

It's my project, but it's not only about churn. The same steps apply to fraud, demand, pricing — anything where a company wants a model it can trust, running every day, at scale.

- **The situation** — the real problem at that point in the build

No Databricks experience needed.

## Where I'm coming from

Nine years in analytics and data science. I've built and shipped a governed, multi-market ML system on Databricks — deployed as config, promoted on a statistical test, monitored for drift, with a GenAI layer on top. I'm now working on scaling it beyond one region.

Some of this is me teaching what I've run in production. Some is me learning in the open. I'll say which is which.

---

## What this is not

- **Not a course.** The value is in the decisions, not the syntax.
- **Not vendor marketing.** Every episode says what it cost, not just what it did.
- **Not confidential.** Everything public uses methods and shapes — never figures, markets, or internal names.
