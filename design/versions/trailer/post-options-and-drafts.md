## Formatting choices

- **Bold only what a skimmer should take away:** the hook, the scale (*eight markets, every day*), the three lessons, the series name and the promise. Everything else stays plain, so the bold still stands out.
- **"Databricks" stays plain in the body.** LinkedIn search reads Unicode bold as different characters, so a bolded 𝗗𝗮𝘁𝗮𝗯𝗿𝗶𝗰𝗸𝘀 won't match anyone searching "Databricks". The plain mention plus the hashtag keeps the post findable. Only the series *name* is bolded.
- **Emoji as markers, not decoration.** 📍🛠️🌏 mark the three seasons and ✅ marks what each episode gives you, so they help people scan.
- **Short lines and white space.** Most LinkedIn reading happens on phones, and dense paragraphs get scrolled past.
- **Screen readers:** Unicode bold is read out letter by letter on some devices. Keeping bold to short phrases limits the impact.
