Three problems — and what solved them
Problem
Dozens of runs, and no memory of which settings produced which score.
→
MLflow experiments
Every run logs its parameters, metrics and the model itself. The bake-off becomes a table you can sort, not a story you remember.
Problem
Churners are rare. A model can look accurate by predicting nobody leaves.
→
scale_pos_weight
One setting tells the model a missed churner costs more. SMOTE added complexity and no gain, so it went.
Problem
One model per market multiplies everything: training, monitoring, explaining.
→
One pooled model
Market becomes a feature, not a separate model. It won on the metric and on simplicity. The bigger reason comes in Episode 8.
The best model is the one you can still retrain, monitor and explain a year from now.
